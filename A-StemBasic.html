Disclaimer

This tool is designed to help conjugate A-Stem (Derived) Verbs in Sindarin. While it strives for accuracy, please be aware that:

  • Complexity of Sindarin: Sindarin is a constructed language with intricate rules, including vowel changes, consonant mutations, and contextual variations. Additionally, some verbs are transitive, some are intransitive, and some can be both. Due to the limitations of the code, this conjugator shows both forms for all verbs, even if a verb is strictly one or the other.
  • Potential for Errors: Despite our best efforts, certain renditions may be incorrect. The complexity of the language and the limitations of algorithmic processing mean that not all rules or vowel changes can be perfectly represented.
  • User Responsibility: It is advisable to cross-reference the results with trusted Sindarin resources or consult with knowledgeable linguists to ensure accuracy, especially for scholarly or formal work.
  • Updates and Feedback: This tool is continuously evolving. If you encounter any errors or have suggestions for improvement, your feedback is highly valued and will contribute to enhancing the accuracy and functionality of this conjugator.
