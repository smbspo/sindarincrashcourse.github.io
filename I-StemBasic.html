The verb car-/caw- debate

The debate around the verb forms car- and caw- stems from attested instances of both, each accompanied by short vowels. This conjugator accounts for those but uses the long ó for the rest of its conjugations:

  • agor "made, did" (WJ/415)
  • agorech "*we have done" 1st PL Inclusive (VT50/21, Noldorin 1949)
  • agorer "*made"; [immediate correction] (VT50/18)
  • cawn "tasted" (PE22/152)
  • agowen "I tasted" (PE22/152)
