Ior Lû/Andrann Vinui

en·Andrann Vinui iesta vo Echui in·Edhelrim v’ În i·Ñgaladhad 1050, ach in·În hain delir vi 1500 (În i·Ñgaladhad) vo echaded Ithil ah Anor vi had nan În Anor. To hath rodhaer nan en·Andrann Vinui bilebra 4,312 coranor fo eriad Anor vinui.
The First Age begins with the Awakening of the Elves in the Years of the Trees 1050, but those years end in 1500 (The year of the Trees) with the forming of the Moon and Sun in place of the Year of the Sun. Thus the most part of the First Age equals 4,312 solar years before the first rising of the Sun.
