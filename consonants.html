The following consonant chart was created by Fiona Jallings and is available in Section 9.3.2, ‘How to Combine Words,’ in her 2017 book, A Fan’s Guide to Neo-Sindarin. The rows represent the ends of words, and the columns represent the beginnings of words. If a space is blank, there’s not enough information to form a hypothesis, and it’s probably best to avoid combining these sounds. Please be mindful of this chart, as new information may have emerged since its creation in 2017, rendering the combination incorrect. Therefore, always double-check to see if there are other examples of known attested formations of those combinations.
